One of our favorite places to eat... The Feed Store in Hemphill, Texas.  Neat setting with a large menu including some healthy delicious meals such as grilled salmon.  I tried out their crawfish on Saturday and it was actually quite yummy!  Hosts and waiters/waitresses are very kind and helpful, AND they have super good milk shakes, deserts and other treats.
