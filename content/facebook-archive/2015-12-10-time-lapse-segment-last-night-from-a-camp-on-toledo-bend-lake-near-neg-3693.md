Time lapse segment last night from a camp on Toledo Bend Lake, near Negreet.  Though we let the camera shoot for two hours, probably only 30 minutes of the segment was good because of the heavy moisture in the air.  We are concocting a device with a large tube sock and hand warmers in hopes of allowing these lapses to continue successfully for longer periods : )  I'll have to wait and see how that works!
