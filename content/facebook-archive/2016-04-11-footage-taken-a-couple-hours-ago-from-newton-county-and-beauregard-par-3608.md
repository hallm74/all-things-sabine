Footage taken a couple hours ago from Newton County and Beauregard Parish on the Sabine River, between Texas and Louisiana (Bon Weir and Merryville).  A beautiful white sandy beach is along the river, after flooding on the Sabine.  The river and "beach" were absolutely stunning and picturesque... Not to mention quiet and peaceful .... Hard to imagine the river just a couple weeks ago had caused so much pain and suffering and destruction by flooding its banks miles in either direction.
