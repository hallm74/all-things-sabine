Then and now... Fisher prior to 1914 and Fisher today.  When the older photo was taken, the Fisher Commissary only included one building, dated 1900.  In 1914, a second building was added.  Oxen pulling logs are shown in the older photo.  Fisher, Louisiana was a logging town, owned and operated by Louisiana Long Leaf Lumber Company, which owned and operated all the buildings and homes in the village.  Fisher incorporated as a Village in the 1970s.  Many of the old homes and buildings are still standing
