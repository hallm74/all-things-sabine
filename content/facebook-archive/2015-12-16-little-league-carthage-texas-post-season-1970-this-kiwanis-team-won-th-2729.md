Little League - Carthage, Texas - post-season 1970
This Kiwanis team won the championship of the Major League in the Carthage, Texas Little League.
Photo from Jim Lambert, who writes, "The smiles may reflect that this photograph was taken after the final game of our championship season.  Now, all we had to look forward to was being individually selected to the Carthage Little League All-Star Team which would represent our town, hopefully, all the way to Williamsport, Pennsylvania and the Little League World Series."
