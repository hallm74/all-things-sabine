The very first passenger train which rolled through Many, in 1896!  The train through Many was operated by KCS and its run through Many caused quite a sensation!  Townspeople gathered in their finest dress to be in the photo taken on that eventful day.  I took smaller snapshots of portions of the photo, both from a photocopy of the photo and a newspaper reprint of the photo, in hopes of providing a bit more detail.
