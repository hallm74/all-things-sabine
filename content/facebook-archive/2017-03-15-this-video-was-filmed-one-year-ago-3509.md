This video was filmed one year ago... in March 2016 and captures the beauty of one area of Hodges Gardens State Park.  It is a three-minute video that shows some of the main garden area... the Gardens themselves are some 700 acres, so this is just a tiny part of Hodges Gardens.  My 16- and 17-year old daughters made this video, as they have a passion and love for Hodges Gardens, which is truly a place for people of all ages...  
Go to Friends of Hodges Gardens Facebook page to see how you can help try to keep the Gardens from shutting down... If people don't stand behind Hodges, it will surely be closed as is planned in just a couple of weeks' time, so shows of support are URGENT!
