For three seasons starting in 2014, there was a TV series on WGN called “Salem” that was filmed in part in DeSoto Parish (due south of Shreveport). An entire village was built in a remote area of land between Mansfield and Grand Cane. Leslie Baker Dews told me about this location recently... I did not know about it. Unfortunately, all the buildings constructed for the set are deteriorating rapidly, as this aerial footage we took last Saturday shows
