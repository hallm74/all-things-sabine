Pine Flat School, 1930.  All photos I am certain are from Pine Flat School except for the last photo, which I just know was in Sabine Parish.  Pine Flat was a close-knit African American community in Southwest Sabine Parish.  The entire community was flooded as a result of the creation of Toledo Bend Lake.  Many of its citizens relocated to the Florien area, specifically a community called Yellow Pine.
