Fisher Sawmill Days coming up in Fisher, Louisiana (Sabine Parish off of U. S. Hwy. 171 south of Many and North of Leesville)

Here is a schedule of events:

2016 Fisher Saw Mill Days Schedule of Events

Friday May 20, 2016

Street Dance from 8p.m. till 10:30 p.m. --- Featuring After Hours 
 Vendors will be open

Saturday May 21, 2016
 9 am –5K walk /run VENDORS OPEN
 10 am –Parade Line up at Fisher Fire Station
 11am—Parade 
 Noon – Opening Ceremony in Village Square
 12:30 – 1:30 Dancers – Village Square
 1:30- 2:30 Cheer Competition
 2:30 – 3:00 MHS Band – Village Square
 3:00 – 4:00 Singing and Award Ceremony
 4:00 – 6:00 – Music in the square by DJ Dewy Eaves
 7pm – 10 pm Dance in the square with music by Straight Shot 

Activities:
 Treasure hunt for $50.00 gift card
 Vendors Food and craft
 Log Weight Guessing 
 Kids Logging Competition -- Log Rolling, Limb Cutting and other events
 Arm Wrestling
 K-9 Demonstration
 Train Rides for the kids

Vendor and garage sale spots still available call 318-602-1531
