From 1970s brochure for Many, La (Sabine Parish). Described as “City Complex”, city hall was to the left on second story and police department to left on main floor. Fire department to right. Today, PD is still on main floor on left building, storage is on second floor, City Hall in right building and fire department is located on Hwy. 6 East (“Natchitoches Hwy”) in Many
