Time lapse segment taken on Toledo Bend last night.  Looking towards the Pendleton Bridge.  Approximately 600 shots in six second exposures were taken to make this.  The outer edge of our Milky Way galaxy can be seen at the right of the frame.  Unfortunately, the cold temperature along with air dense with smoke from a fire a few miles away fogged my lens into the time lapse.  Shooting time was two hours.  I need a good location to shoot on the lake where my camera will be secure and I can leave it!  Any suggestions?
