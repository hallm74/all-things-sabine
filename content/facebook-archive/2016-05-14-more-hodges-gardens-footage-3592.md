More Hodges Gardens footage!  Hope we aren't boring anyone with this because it sure is fun to shoot and produce... And because we want as many people as possible to know that this treasure is in fact endangered as the State of Louisiana has included it in a list of parks to be closed.  I strongly believe in the power of the people, and I hope the people will speak out and plea with any state officials or friends of powers that be... Etc... To keep Hodges Gardens open!  #keephodgesopen.  This video is a bit over five minutes and was filmed from one of the lower areas of the main gardens.
