Shared by Dale Stagg:  The old historic Sabine Lighthouse in Cameron Parish (across the river and ship channel from Sabine Pass). The light was extinguished for the last time in 1952. The Sabine Pass Lighthouse - Cameron Preservation Alliance is working to try to preserve the foundation of this lighthouse to keep it from being destroyed completely.
