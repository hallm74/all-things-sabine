The old Sabine River Bridge, called Gaines Memorial Bridge, which crossed the Sabine River between Texas and Louisiana.  The following information is from BridgeHunter.Com:

The bridge was demolished during the construction of Toledo Bend Reservoir and only the piers remain inundated by the Reservoir.
