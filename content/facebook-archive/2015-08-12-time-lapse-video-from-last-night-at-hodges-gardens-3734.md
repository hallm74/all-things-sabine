Time lapse video from last night at Hodges Gardens... camera took exposures all night long and into the morning.  There are a lot of airplanes, some moving cloud cover, and yes, some meteors!  Hard to catch the latter and you have to watch close to spot them but they are there.  Towards the end, there is some lightning activity, and then the sun begins to rise at the very end.  My camera was shooting straight to the northern sky.  About a third of the way through, there is a meteor and then right after that an interesting flash, kind of across the center of the frame.  Just after the flash, there appears to be a bit of smoke produced which rises upward a bit.  It perplexed me for a while... I slowed down the video and looked closer.  This is in the next video...
