Sabine Parish men and women again answer call to duty. A very nice article penned by Rickey Robertson, of Peason. Robertson is an historian, particularly in areas of Military nature.  He offers plenty of interesting information and details in this article, including one single family- the Remedies family, from which five members answered this important call during this critical time of World War 2.
