November 1967.  Workmen place floats under the old Pendleton (Sabine River) Bridge in preparation to float the old structure to the shore so it could be dismantled.  This photo was taken from the new Pendleton Bridge over the new Toledo Bend Lake.  The new bridge towered over this old bridge.
