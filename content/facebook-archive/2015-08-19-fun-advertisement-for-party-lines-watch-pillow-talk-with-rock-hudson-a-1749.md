Fun advertisement for party lines (watch Pillow Talk with Rock Hudson and Doris Day and you will get the gist of what a party line was!) from a Many 1950s phone book that Miss Sarah Vandegaer had saved and shared a few years ago. Once upon a time, people actually had to share their phone lines (only land lines back then!). Hard to imagine now. Oh, and you had to rent your actually telephone from the phone company ... Plus pay the monthly service. I'm guessing even with that it was still phenomenally cheaper than today's phone bills.
