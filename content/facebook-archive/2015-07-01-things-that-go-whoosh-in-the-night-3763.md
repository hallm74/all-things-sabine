Things that Go Whoosh in the Night...
Time lapse I took on June 13 (before this new moon) at Hodges Gardens for about six hours just prior to daylight.  You can see stellar activity, changing cloud activity, aviation activity,  even part of an outer edge of our Milky Way Galaxy (in the early part of the video), and then, ultimately, solar activity as the sun rises over Hodges Lake.
