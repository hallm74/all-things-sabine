Then... and now.  Photo taken today merged with photo taken in early 1900s.  Fisher, Louisiana.  By the Fisher Train depot.  Suited men await the arrival of the Kansas City Southern passenger train in the old photo.  In the new photo, the Fisher train depot still stands as a testament of the past... but only freight trains pass through today.  Oh, and I've yet to see a pig roaming town.
