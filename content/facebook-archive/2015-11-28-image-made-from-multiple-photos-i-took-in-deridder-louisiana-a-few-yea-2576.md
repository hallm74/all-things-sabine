Image made from multiple photos I took in DeRidder, Louisiana a few years ago... I bracketed photos and combined them old school to make what is now referred to as an HDR (high dynamic range) photo.  These can no be taken and composed automatically with a click of a button.  A few years ago, it was a far more tedious process :)  The lightning was added on the back end... There was lightning at the time I shot these, but it was behind us so I took the liberty of adding the effect.

#DeRidderLouisiana
