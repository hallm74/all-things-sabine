The full moon of the last night two nights has been spectacular and I have taken a few photos of which Im anxious to see the outcome and will share if they are decent. In the meantime, this is a full moon (well, almoooooosssst full) I photographed on Toledo Bend Lake three or four summers ago (when the lake was really low, as in record low, below 161 ft msl). I caught the moon in the early early morning hours (around 6 am if I remember correctly). I combined a few pix I took that morning along with a long exposure I took the previous evening to get this image.
