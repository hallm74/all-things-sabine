I love the outfits and the poses!!!  Super dapper, they were...  In front of the Pelican Store in Zwolle. Not sure of the year of these photos but all of what I see in them gives clues that this is really old... As in at least a century. I love the last photo of the guys inside the store. Wow, what a crew... Business must have been very good for the drug stores of this day. I notice that there are lots of men hanging out in the store with lots of photos and images of lots of women gracing the counters and walls.
