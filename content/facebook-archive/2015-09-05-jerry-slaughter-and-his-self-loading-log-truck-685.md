Jerry Slaughter and his "self loading" log truck. Mr. slaughter's daughter, Suzanne Craft, was kind enough to share these photos in response to the interest generated on a previous photo she had shared. Photo taken in April 1975. The second photo shows a truck that Mr. Slaughter drove, in front of their house on Plainview Road. Photo was taken in the 1970s. Shown from left are Suzanne's cousin, Gary Slaughter; grandfather, John Carmen Slaughter; father, Jerry Slaughter; and uncle, Johnnie Slaughter. #1975SabineParish
