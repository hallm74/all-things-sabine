Cleco helping put Christmas lights up in Many in 1967. In the first photo, they are in front of where All Hours Fitness is today... On the pole hard at work are George Strickland, Tommy Koss and George Faust. On the ground from left, are Aubrey Gates and Jackie Savell of Cleco; and Hildo Remedies, Woodrow Williams and Wiley White working for the Town of Many, and Wayne Bostian of Cleco.  In the second photo, which was taken in Zwolle (where Cleco also helped put up lights), Strickland is atop the pole. On the ground from left are Koss, Savell, Milton McFerrin, and Bostian, of Cleco. Details in the third image.
