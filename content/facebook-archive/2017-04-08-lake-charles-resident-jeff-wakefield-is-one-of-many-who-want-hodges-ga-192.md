Lake Charles resident Jeff Wakefield is one of many who want Hodges Gardens State Park to stay open to the public.  "I would like to try to save this Heavenly place from closing," Wakefield told us in a message which was accompanied by these beautiful photographs he took at Hodges.  He is sharing these photos to help show the beauty of Hodges Gardens to those who haven't visited in a while, or who live outside of our geographic area and are unfamiliar with Hodges Gardens.  Please take a moment to help us with a grass roots effort to get the word out to state lawmakers that we want to see Hodges Gardens saved as a park for the public to enjoy and cherish for many years to come... You can help by adding your name to a petition we will be sending to lawmakers... it doesn't matter where you live, any one any where of any age can add their name to the petition, which can be found by clicking this link:
http://allthingssabine.com/keep-hodges-gardens-state-park-open/
And thanks so much to Jeff Wakefield for sharing these photographs.
