We had the privilege of shooting last night at a ranch in Many, thanks to Dr. Robert Brocato.  The view from his ranch offered an expansive magnificent view of the night sky... a spectacular view that is must be as close to a photographer's dream in the Southern Central U.S. and Southeastern U.S. as one could find.  This time lapse was taken of the southern sky using a fish eye lens and 13 seconds shots taken throughout the night.  You can see the spiral arm of the Milky Way closest to the galactic core throughout the seven hour segment.
