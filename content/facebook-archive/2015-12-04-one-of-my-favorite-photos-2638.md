One of my favorite photos... Taken some two years ago at Hodges Gardens.  All I had at the time was my handy Iphone 4 to snap this with.  My son saw a huge grass carp and as my son can be impulsive, literally hopped out of the canoe and scooped up the big fish.  I was on the shore telling the kids to head back to Canoe Point and park the canoe.  As you can see, my daughter was fairly shocked by the event.  My son immediately released the carp and he swam off (we actually think he might have been sick because he was moving slowly enough to be scooped up by a human kid and because when the human kid let him go, he swam a few yards away and just lingered there for a while, doing nothing).
