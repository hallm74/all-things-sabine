1925, Leesville Tire & Vulcanizing Shop, located at North Third Avenue and Lee Street.  D. B. Rowzee, owner, is shown at left.  He owned and introduced the first steam valcanizing in Vernon Parish.  
Boone Rowzee married Donna S. Self (4/22/1888 - 3/30/1968)

This was a Gulf Service Station.

Photo from the Vernon Parish Library.
