Online petition to show support of Hodges Gardens State Park:
http://allthingssabine.com/keep-hodges-gardens-state-park-open/

All Things Sabine has created an online petiton in hopes of further showing public support of keeping Hodges Gardens open.  If the State of Louisiana does not provide funding for it in the coming fiscal year's budget (fiscal year begins July 1), then Hodges Gardens will close.  We know there is plenty of support for Hodges Gardens, as tens of thousands of people have viewed our posts on Hodges.  Please just take a moment to complete this very simple petiton... Brief comments are optional.  Email addresses are required but will only be used for the purposes of this petition (this is a petition hosted only on our secure server).  Physical addresses are not required but we do encourage participants to fill in the city and state in which they reside (ANYONE can complete this petiton, regardless of where you live!). If you love Hodges, please participate and show your support!  Please message me if you have any questions or problems with this petition.  

Again, a link to the petition:  http://allthingssabine.com/keep-hodges-gardens-state-park-open/
