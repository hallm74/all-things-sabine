It is rare that you can catch the sun and moon in the same shot... this one I took at Hodges Gardens about three years ago.  I have been watching since for another opportunity to shoot the two together, but none have presented to me yet.  There have been some where I can catch the two in the same wide shot, but none since where they were this close in appeared proximity.
