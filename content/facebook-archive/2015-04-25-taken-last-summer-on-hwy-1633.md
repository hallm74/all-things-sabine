Taken last summer on Hwy. 171 in Many... Mary Brocato's hay barn. This is a real photo!  I took a long detailed exposure of the barn itself and a fraction of a second of the moon (yes, it was huge and yellow!) and then photos of the entire scene at low aperture for 30 seconds, one minute and two minutes and merged all photos into one. I am hoping summer brings us some more incredible moons!
