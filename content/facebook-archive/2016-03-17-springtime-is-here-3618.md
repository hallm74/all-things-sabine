Springtime is here.  A five minute look at Hodges Gardens beginning to bloom.  Hodges Gardens State Park is located in Sabine Parish, Louisiana, off of U. S. Hwy. 171.  
Admission to the park is $5 per person, $4 for seniors 62+ and children under 3 are admitted free. We take advantage of the annual pass because the passes are $80 (covers any one in the car!) and can be used at any state park.  Ours get A LOT of use!
The gates open at 8AM daily and close at 9PM Sunday through Thursday and 10PM on Friday and Saturday. The Gardens close at sunset.
