Panola County Courthouse in 1940s
Or 50s. This courthouse was built in the 1880s and was a twin to the courthouse in the southern contiguous Parish of Shelby. In 1953, the courthouse was torn down and another courthouse built... This one more modern and less architecturally significant. The courthouse is located in the county seat of Carthage.
