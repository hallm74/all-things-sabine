Many Lumber Company and Sawmill, owned and operated by John A. Hoagland and Dr. S. E. Cade from 1905 until 1916. The sawmill was first located in the Recknor Community west of Many on Texas Road. Next it was located on Hoagland's farm about four miles east of Many on the Middle Creek Road. The final location of the mill was in the New Hope Community.
