Vandegaer Abstract was operated by W. H. Vandegaer and his daughter, Sarah Vandegaer, shown in the photo. The business was located above Peoples State Bank in downtown Many (currently the second floor of K & W Clothing). Photo was taken in 1920s or maybe 1930s.
