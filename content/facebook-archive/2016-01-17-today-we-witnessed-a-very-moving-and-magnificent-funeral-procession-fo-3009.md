Today, we witnessed a very moving and magnificent funeral procession for John Franklin Lewis, a founding member of the Gray Ghosts Motorcycle Club. Lewis was buried this afternoon at St. John the Baptist Church Cemetery, with over 500 fellow motorcyclists participating in an awesome tribute to him. We were truly honored to be asked to capture footage of the procession and are eagerly finalizing a video for the family and friends.
