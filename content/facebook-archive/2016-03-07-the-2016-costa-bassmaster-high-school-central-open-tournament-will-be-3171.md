The 2016 Costa Bassmaster High School Central Open tournament will be held March 12 on Toledo Bend Lake.  It will be hosted out of Cypress Bend by the Sabine Parish Tourist Commission- Toledo Bend Lake Country in partnership with B. A. S. S. Nation. 

High school anglers competed in this event out of Cypress Bend last year.  One hundred and seventeen teams participated.

The tournament will be from 6 a.m. til 6 p.m.
