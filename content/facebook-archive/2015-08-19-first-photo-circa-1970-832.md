First photo:  circa 1970. Original kitchen house at Fort Jessup near Many, Louisiana in Sabine Parish. Also known as Fort Jesup or Fort Jesup State Monument. This kitchen is the only remaining historical structure at the site.
Second, 1970s, Fort Jesup (established 1822), located 5.6 miles Northeast of Many, La., on Hwy. 3118. Sabine Parish. 
Third and fourth photos:  Entry fence at Fort Jesup State Park and Museum in Sabine Parish Louisiana in 1968
