Star Trails galore.  Taken last night, looking towards the Dippers, at Hodges Gardens.  One 30 second exposure taken every minute for a few hours (until the battery died).  In all, 144 photos taken, stacked in layers, and blended to show the full spectrum of the trails of the stars' as well as the spiral arm of the Milky Way.  I guess based on those numbers, the total time of shooting was 216 minutes, or almost four hours.
