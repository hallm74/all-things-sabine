A really long time ago. In the courtroom of the courthouse before the courthouse that is today's courthouse. This particular courthouse burned in 1916. Shown are Judge Don E. Sorelle, and on his right is Sheriff T.J. Cranford, while on his left is William Vandegaer, Clerk of Court.  Seated in front from left are R. A. Fraser, attorney; two unidentified, District Attorney Robinson, attorney Goode, Mr. Lyles, assistant district attorney, next unidentified and at far right is George Pierce.
