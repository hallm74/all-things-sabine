Video footage taken this afternoon at the Toledo Bend Dam.  9 of 11 spillway gates are open 22 feet each (two of the 11 gates were under repair),  releasing more than 200,000 cubic feet of water per second... Water is released first into Toro Creek and then it quickly makes its way to the Sabine River, and down towards Southwest Louisiana and Southeast Texas.  The video is fairly raw and unedited, to enable us to get this up quickly as we could, so it is six minutes of footage.
