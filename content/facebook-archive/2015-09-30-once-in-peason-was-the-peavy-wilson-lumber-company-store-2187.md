Once in Peason was the Peavy Wilson Lumber Company store. Peavy Wilson owned and operated Peason (named for, appropriately, Peavy Wilson). Photo taken in 1917. Talk about a one stop shop... You could find merchandise of all kinds, the Post office, the drug store, and even the Doctor's offices here.
