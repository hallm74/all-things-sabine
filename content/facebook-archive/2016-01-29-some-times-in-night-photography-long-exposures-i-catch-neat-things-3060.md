Some times, in night photography (long exposures), I catch neat things.  The light to the right, above the horizon, is one of those.  Have no idea what it is.  It is not an airplane... I caught plenty of airplanes last night in a series of photos I took at Clyde's Crossing in Zwolle, Louisiana.... airplanes produce a dotted light pattern because the lights on them are blinking.  Also, I've never seen an airplane produce a prismatic streak such as this one.  I'm not saying this is a UFO or anything... probably a very simple and logical explanation.  I just don't happen to have a clue as to what that explanation is.
