"Sparrow" McClanahan was a popular cool at the American Cafe located in downtown Many. He is shown here at the rear of the cafe. I believe the cafe was on the south side of San Antonio but not certain. Photo was taken in the 1930s and shared by Patsy Knippers for a book we did on Many history.
