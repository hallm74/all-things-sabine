Today, while we were on business at All Hours Fitness in Center, Texas, we were fortunate to catch a photo shoot in progress for the upcoming Timpson Frontier Days (Shelby County).  Ladies and gentlemen, decked out in proper Texas Western attire, and mounted on horses, were cleverly posing for photos in front of the old Shelby County Courthouse, built back in the 1880s.  
The crew was kind enough to oblige my perhaps unusual photo opp request and they docked their horses on the old iron rings which are still affixed to the retaining wall in front of the courthouse.  Thanks for being good sports :)
