The old jail in Zwolle, Louisiana.  Oh, if only these walls could talk, what tales they would tell!  A tiny and yet oh so eerie brick building with four 2x2' bar covered window openings and two bar covered doors.  Brick toilet in each of the two tiny sections of the jail.  Photos taken last night, since it was kind of one of those let's go find an old scary building to take photographs of nights.
