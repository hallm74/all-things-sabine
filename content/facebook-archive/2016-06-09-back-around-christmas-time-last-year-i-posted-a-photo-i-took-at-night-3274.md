Back around Christmas time last year, I posted a photo I took at night of the Rio Theater in Center, Texas.  We took an aerial photo of the super cool theater while we were in Center earlier this week.  I'm posting this photo partially because I like the photo, partially because the Rio is a really cool building architecture-wise and is a vintage theater still in full operation which plays new movies and everything... But mostly, I am posting this photo because the photo we posted in December was one of the most popular photos we had ever posted.  In all, it reached over one million people and had over 5,000 likes... And it generated countless positive comments... Not just about the theater itself, but about the owners of the theater who must be absolutely incredible people because they clearly are adored and appreciated in Shelby County and beyond, as evidenced by the many, many, many comments and kind words spoken of them!
