Another couple of old Many High School.  I have more information... It was the first high school in the parish to be approved by the State Board of Education, in 1909.  Was a two story wood frame building, erected in 1906 at a cost of $3,500.  Before that building, the old Presbyterian Church at East Main and Ash served as a school in Many.  
The building had three rooms on the first floor-- one for high school grade students, one for grammar school and one for primary grade students.  Upstairs were an auditorium and a rear room for a lab that boasted nearly $300 of equipment.  There was a water well located behind the schoolhouse and a woodpile from which kindling was chopped for building fires.
