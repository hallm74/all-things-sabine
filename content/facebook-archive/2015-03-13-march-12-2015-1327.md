March 12, 2015... Hwy 171, watched frustrated as unmarked truck pulling a trailer with a load that extended well beyond one lane (oversize load signage on back barely visible) posed danger to vehicular traffic  including myself. This was at 10 pm in a steady drizzle and heavy fog and oh by the way oversize loads are prohibited on state highways after dark.
