At Hodges Gardens... Perseid meteor shower... one from early this morning (5 a.m. to about 545 a.m) and the other taken from about around 11 a.m. until 2 a.m.  The first is a composite of 88 frames in which three had meteors and the second a composite of a few hundred frames (just shy of 500) in which eight had meteors.  I stacked the exposures with the meteors with one without any meteor activity and created masks for each of the meteors.  I was hoping to improve on it all tonight, knowing a little more than I did last night... but I think heavy cloud cover may stop renewed efforts.
