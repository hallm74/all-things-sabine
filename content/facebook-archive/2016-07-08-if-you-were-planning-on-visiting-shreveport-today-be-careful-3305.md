If you were planning on visiting Shreveport today, be careful.  Hopefully nothing will come of this, but flyers are being distributed to encourage a riot at the Caddo Courthouse at 10 this morning.  "Let's stand together and fight back... Let's start a riot by the courthouse in Shreveport" is what is stated in the flyer.  The language is frightening, to say the least... "Y'all wanted to purge so bad, let's give them one and tear s$hit down."
