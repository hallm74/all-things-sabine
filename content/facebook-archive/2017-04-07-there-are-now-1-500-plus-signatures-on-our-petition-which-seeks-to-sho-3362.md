There are now 1,500-plus signatures on our petition which seeks to show Louisiana lawmakers public support of keeping Hodges Gardens State Park open.  
Click here to see the petition:  http://allthingssabine.com/keep-hodges-gardens-state-park-open/
While I am quite pleased with the number of signatures, I still believe there is plenty more support out there for Hodges, and remember, there is strength in numbers. I really want to send a super strong message to the State that we want Hodges to stay around as a place to be cherished and enjoyed by the public.  So please, if you haven't taken a moment (it takes no more than a minute) to add your name to the petition, please consider doing so.  Thank you!
