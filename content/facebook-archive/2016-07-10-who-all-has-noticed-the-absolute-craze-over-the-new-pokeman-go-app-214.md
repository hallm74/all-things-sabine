Who all has noticed the ABSOLUTE CRAZE over the new Pokeman Go app.  Every single where I go, kids and adults alike are walking around, staring at their phones, exclaiming things like, "I found it!" "It's over there" "We're close" etc etc etc.  Scenes of madness, every where!  Yesterday, we were in Lake Charles... This was happening everywhere.  Friday evening, we saw it all over Many.  Today, we are in Shreveport, and again, every where.  I love the way one grown "kid" of about age 20 in Lake Charles explained it to me yesterday... "Nerds from all over are coming out of their houses, myself included!"  He added, "This is amazing... I have seen nerds running, at night... You just don't see that!"
