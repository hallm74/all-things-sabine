Kinder Motor Company letter to J. H. Burns in Slagle, Louisana, 1925:
"We will try and be ready to deliver the touring cars ... next week.  We will suggest that you try and sell both of these parties on balloon tires, however, if it is impossible to sell them balloon tires, I would like for both to take demountable rims if possible because there is a shortage of clincher wheels."
From Vernon Parish Library
