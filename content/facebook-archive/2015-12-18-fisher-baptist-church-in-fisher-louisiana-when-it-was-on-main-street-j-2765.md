Fisher Baptist Church in Fisher, Louisiana, when it was on Main Street, just before you reach South Second Avenue.  The main part of the church was moved in the 1990s to Village Square, where it is preserved and still stands.  After the old building was moved, a new brick building was constructed for use by the congregation of the Fisher Baptist Church.
