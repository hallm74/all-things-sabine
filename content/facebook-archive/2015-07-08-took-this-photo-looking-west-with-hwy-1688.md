Took this photo looking west, with Hwy. 171 north of Fisher running in front of me.  During an odd storm which was very low lying, producing lots of lightning but no rain (at least not in our direction).  The streaks at the bottom are the traffic trails (tail and head lights) and the whiter light further into the picture is the light of a KCS train traveling south to north.  Photo was taken 3-4 weeks ago, before Jupiter and Venus's intersect (you can see both planets in the picture)
