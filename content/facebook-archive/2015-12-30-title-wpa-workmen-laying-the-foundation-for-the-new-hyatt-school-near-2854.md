Title WPA workmen laying the foundation for the new Hyatt school near Fields, Louisiana in the 1930s
Subject United States. Works Progress Administration of Louisana

At the time of this photograph, A well has already been dug for the school's water supply system and an electric and heating plant is to be installed in the school's basement. WPA workmen will build a principal's cottage, janitor's home, teacher's home, manual training school shop and home economics cottage. The project will cost approximately $192,500.
