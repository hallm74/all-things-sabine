From Louisiana State Library. 1970s. Sawmill Days Festival in Fisher Louisiana. Written on photo: Sawmill Days takes place in Fisher Louisiana on U.S. 171 south of Many. The homes, picket fences, commissary, opera house and depot of this turn-of-the-century sawmill town, founded in 1897, have been preserved, postcard perfect. The village forms the backdrop for the annual Sawmill Days Festival, featuring contests related to the logging industry, alongside a country fair.
