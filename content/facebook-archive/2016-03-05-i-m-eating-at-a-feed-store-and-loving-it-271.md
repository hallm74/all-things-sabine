I'm eating at a feed store and loving it!  Found this great restaurant in Hemphill, Texas. The Feed Store has a nice menu from burgers and sandwiches to salmon and Alfredo ... Soup of the day (today is squash), Homemade potato chips are served prior to your meal. Everything is charmingly basic here ... Except the food which is far from basic. Nice atmosphere ... Good light music... Exposed brick walls... Menu items and specials on chalkboards mounted to the wall... Nice staff, a bar... I will definitely be eating at this feed store again!
