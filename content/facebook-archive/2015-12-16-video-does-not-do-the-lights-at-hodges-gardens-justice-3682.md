Video does not do the lights at Hodges Gardens justice.  The lights and decorations are fantastic and it is magical to tour through the park and see everything at night.  Know that Hodges Gardens went unlighted at Christmas time for many years... Manager Kim Kelly and staff as well as helpers from other agencies decided to bring the lights back a couple years ago and they all work hard and long hours to bring this Holiday Spirit to people of this area and visitors to this area.  Enjoy this preview of the lights... and stop by Hodges Gardens off Hwy. 171 south of Florien, Louisiana between 6 and 8 p.m. to see it all live and in person.
