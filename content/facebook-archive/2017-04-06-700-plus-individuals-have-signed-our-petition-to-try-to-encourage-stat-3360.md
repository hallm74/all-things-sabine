700-plus individuals have signed our petition to try to encourage State Legislators to fund Hodges Gardens State Park and keep it open.  I know beyond a doubt that there is plenty more support out there for Hodges, so please take a moment to add your name to our petition and help us try to save this gem of a place.

Click here to go to the petiton:  http://allthingssabine.com/keep-hodges-gardens-state-park-open/

Shown here, the outer edge of our Milky Way galaxy, beautifully visible from Hodges Gardens State Park.  I have yet to find a more magical place from which to take photographs of the night sky.  I and many others like myself would miss Hodges tremendously if it were gone.
