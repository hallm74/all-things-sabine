This is a really neat area of Zwolle, Louisiana I discovered a few months ago.  It is "on" Toledo Bend Lake... At San Miguel Creek or Bayou (from Hwy. 191 to Ebarb Road and just a hop, skip and jump down Ebarb Road (maybe just a hop and skip).  We filmed here for Bruce Malmay of Zwolle on Sunday (Malmay is a property developer) so he could see how the water levels in the area looked following the flooding on Toledo Bend.  Part of this land is a very lightly developed subdivision called "Nantucket" while much of the rest of the property remains undeveloped.
