I have seen a significant (over 40) number of photos of the gentleman in this photograph, Festus Keelen of Zwolle, Louisiana.  And in many, possibly most, of them, he is surrounded by women.  I have decided based on this, that he must have been a highly sought after man.  So I couldn't resist the inclination to hone in on his face and see if his looks played a part in this.  They did.  What a good looking guy.  I suppose it didn't hurt that he was also very wealthy, a dapper dresser, and probably quite charming as well.
