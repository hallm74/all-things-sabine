Who remembers Henry the Ram and the buffalo from Hodges Gardens many years ago?  Both were at the Gardens until the property became a State Park. Henry lived on House Island (where the Hodges house was) and kept the lawn maintained. The buffalo lived in a large pasture in the park. I do not know what became of the ram or the buffalo.
