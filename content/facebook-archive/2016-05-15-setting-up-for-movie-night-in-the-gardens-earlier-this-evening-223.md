Setting up for movie night in the Gardens earlier this evening. The Yogi Bear Movie was the feature and by showtime, at least a couple hundred people... Mostly children and families... Had shown up to take advantage of the treat of enjoying a movie in Hodges Gardens Park. People brought blankets and pillows and other feature comforts. Hodges shows a few feature movies a year as well as offers all kinds of other neat events for all different age groups. #keephodgesopen
