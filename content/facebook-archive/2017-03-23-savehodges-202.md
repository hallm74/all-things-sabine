#savehodges .... Public meeting tonight at Sabine Theater in Many, at 5:30 pm..
Hodges Gardens is a true FAMILY destination ... Some of my fondest memories have been made at Hodges Gardens. I and my family have spent so much time at Hodges that is like a second home to us. I am a fairly private person ... and this is a big step for me to share these photos with so many people, but I am passionate enough about Hodges and what it means to me and to many other families to share these... many viewers may find them too great in number ... I think there are 30 photos... but I was having trouble narrowing them down. I discovered I have over 20,000 photos taken at Hodges alone by my exif data my cameras store... so maybe 30 isn't so bad :). I hope to get the message that this Park is an absolute gem in our area... we truly need it... please, if you are able, stop by the Sabine Theater in Many tonight (5:30pm) to show your support for Hodges Gardens. I will even be speaking... I am an awful public speaker but hope I will be able to blunder through my stage fright and get my message across... which is... WE NEED HODGES GARDENS!
