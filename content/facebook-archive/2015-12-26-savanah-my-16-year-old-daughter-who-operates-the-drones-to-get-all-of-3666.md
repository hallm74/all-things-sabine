Savanah, my 16 year old daughter who operates the "drones" to get all of the aerial footage you see on All Things Sabine, is on her way to becoming a licensed pilot. She has been flying for more than a year, under an instructor from the Toledo Bend Lake area, has completed over 25 hours of instruction (in flight) and has completed two solo flights. Very proud!  Here she is flying out of the airport in Many, Louisiana. Amelia Lucas, who works with us, filmed this.
