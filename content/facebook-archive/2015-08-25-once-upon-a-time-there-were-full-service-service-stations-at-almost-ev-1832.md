Once upon a time, there were full service service stations at almost every corner.  Today, there are pretty much zero.  Shown here, Cities Service Station was located at the current site of Foy Motors, on San Antonio Avenue in Many. Photo was taken in hte 1940s.  From left, are Bob Howard, R. G. Risor, Leanard Falkner, Boots Muse, and a traveling salesman.  This photo had been shared with me a few years ago by Helen Risor Lane, who was a joy to get to know and a wonderful woman.
