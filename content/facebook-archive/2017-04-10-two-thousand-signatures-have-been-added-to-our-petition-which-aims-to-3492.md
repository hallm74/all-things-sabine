Two thousand signatures have been added to our petition which aims to show public support for Hodges Gardens State Park in southern Sabine Parish, Louisiana.  Click here to view and/or add your signature to the petiton:  http://allthingssabine.com/keep-hodges-gardens-state-park-open/

Hodges Gardens is in danger of closing if the State of Louisiana does not in coming weeks allocate funding for Hodges Gardens to operate.  Two thousand signatures is a lot, but I strongly believe there are so many more supporters out there... If adding an email to the petition concerns you, please try not to let it.. the petition is hosted on our private server and you will not be added to any email lists!  Please consider trying to help save this park... I believe it is important that the caring public try whatever means they can to make a difference in this park's future and signing this petition will just take a moment but will make a statement to lawmakers as they determine whether or not to fund the park.  

Hodges Gardens is one of our favorite locations from which to shoot photography and to film aerial projects... including the video in this post which was taken from "Lookout Point" as we flew our drone north/northwest
