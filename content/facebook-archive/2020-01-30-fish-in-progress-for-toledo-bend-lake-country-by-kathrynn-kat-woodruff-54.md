Fish in progress for Toledo Bend Lake Country by Kathrynn “Kat” Woodruff. Kat wrote in a post today, “A few hours left on this side and then we flip.  Working on artwork for the public brings a whole new level of added pressure.  Usually at the end of a commission I get to talk to my patrons and confirm that they are happy with what I’ve done.  With this all I can do is put my whole heart into it and hope it’s received well by the majority.  I love this parish and I’m honored to be contributing to the aesthetic of it. #sabineproud”
