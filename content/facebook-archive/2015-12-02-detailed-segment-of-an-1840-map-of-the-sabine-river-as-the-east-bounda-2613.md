Detailed segment of an 1840 map of the Sabine River, as
The east boundary of Texas and west boundary of Louisiana. This particular section of river borders Shelby County to the east and DeSoto Parish (Logansport) to the west and extends down to Gaines Ferry (Sabine County / Sabine Parish) at current area of Pendleton Bridge
