Amanda Swatling shared this photo, believed to have been taken in Fisher, La (Sabine Parish) in 1928
She writes, "Here's one (Summer 1928) that has my grandmother - Eleanor Chambers - and others enjoying their Cokes.  She is 2nd from left.  I believe the two kids are my uncle J.B. Chambers and Aunt Sally (all are deceased).  I don't know who the other people are."
