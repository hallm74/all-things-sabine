Mrs. Juanita Miller Anthony, who was the daughter of Ivy Devesta "Dess" Miller, was interviewed in this January 2000 article published in The Sabine Index. The article was titled "Dess Community named after faithful postmaster". This article was brought to our attention by Mrs Juanita's great grandson, Chris Welch, who reports Mrs. Juanita will be turning 95 this month, on Nov. 30. She is living in Southeast Texas.
