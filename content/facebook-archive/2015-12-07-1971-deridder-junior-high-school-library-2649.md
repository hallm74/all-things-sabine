1971, DeRidder Junior High School Library. 
From Louisiana State Library. Written on photo: Students listen to a recording in the library in preparation for a committee meeting. Left to Right: Steve Everett, Michael Fritz, Matthew Lee Jr., and George Ackerman. Student aide Sheila Burgess shelves books in the background. The library received the Louisiana Library Association's Modisette award for most improved school library.
